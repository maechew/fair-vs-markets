Notes

  • Fair. Predicted Democratic share of the two-party House vote, % (), standard error points, treated as normal. House page. Vote data from the Clerk of the House (p. 15).
  • House markets. Kalshi and Polymarket popular vote margin contracts. Democrats win the vote when the margin is above zero; a margin of m points is about 50 + m/2 percent of the two-party vote. Control of the House depends on seats, not votes.
  • Senate. Under Fair's ranking assumption, the chance of control is the chance in the pivot, the race that gives Democrats their 51st seat. Independents count as Democrats. Senate page.
  • Calculator. Changes only inflation and the count of strong-growth quarters (each counts 15/7 toward Zcc); everything else stays at Fair's values. Uses the equation's coefficients rounded to three decimals, so results can differ from Fair's by a few hundredths.
  • Prices. Midpoint of bid and ask, scaled to 100% per race. Chart shading is the bid–ask range. "24h" is the change in Kalshi's price. PredictIt's fees push long-shot prices up.
